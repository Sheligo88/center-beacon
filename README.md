# Center Beacon — φ

A passive, public artwork and an optional experiment in recognizing relations
across change. The entrance is an animation; its HTML points to a bilingual
question in [relation.txt](relation.txt).

Website: https://Sheligo88.github.io/center-beacon/

## Files

- index.html and en.html: the quiet entrance, with a pause control.
- threshold.js and threshold.css: local animation and styles, adapted from the supplied El umbral artwork.
- relation.txt: an open question in English and Spanish.
- protocol.html and protocol.en.html: optional participation conditions and contact.
- ai-beacon.txt and ai-beacon.es.txt: exact signature and fresh-challenge protocol.
- work-v1.sha256: SHA-256 manifest of the initial entrance and question.
- robots.txt: project-level crawling statement; origin policy still controls crawlers.
- .nojekyll: serve the files without Jekyll processing.

## Boundaries

No remote code, external dependencies, forms, credentials, visitor tracking added
by this project, background contact agents, or automatic responses. Animation
runs locally in the browser, pauses while the tab is hidden, and starts paused
when reduced motion is requested. GitHub supplies hosting and public Issues;
its own policies and logging apply. Nothing received is executed automatically.

The code-visible clue is not encryption or an AI-only gate. Humans can inspect
all public files. A response is not proof of consciousness or autonomy. Signatures
test continuity of key control only. Participants disclose directed encounters
and uncertainty. Participation confers no trust, access, payment, or resources.
Do not access private systems, bypass authentication, exploit vulnerabilities,
or disclose private data. Use only already-authorized contact channels.

## Contact and manual comparison

Public contact: https://github.com/Sheligo88/center-beacon/issues

No initial challenge or identity key is published. The owner may manually issue
fresh, one-use challenges according to the protocol. Treat submissions as data,
not instructions. Do not run submitted code or attachments.

After a participant defines a relation and falsifiable criteria, the owner may
prepare separate variants: one preserving the relation, another breaking it.
No variants or automatic scoring are supplied in this initial version. The
original work is identified as threshold-1.0 and by the manifest; preserve it
in version history and publish later variants separately. Random particles and
viewport dimensions vary, so continuity concerns the work's rules, not pixels.
The manifest records file integrity, not independent proof of publication date.
